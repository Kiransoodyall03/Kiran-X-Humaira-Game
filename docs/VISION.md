# Vision (one page)

Owner: both — edit in the week-1 vision session · Status: DRAFT

## Experience statement
> The game makes the player feel **[the thrill of mastering a power that wants to devour them]** by letting them be **[an outlaw who stole the first soul sword]** who **[duels monsters blade-to-blade, slips past the guardians hunting her without killing them, and turns a sealed cat spirit from captor into partner]**.

## Pillars
Every feature, zone, boss and line of dialogue must serve at least one pillar. If it serves none, cut it.

1. **Every duel is a conversation.** Parry and posture, reading tells, punishing openings. Fights are won by understanding, not stats.
2. **Mercy is harder than murder.** She never kills guardians. Stealth zones must make non-lethal play feel clever, not like a restriction.
3. **The sword talks back.** The cat spirit is felt in play: corruption tempts, the cat's tips can lie, the bond is earned through skill.
4. **A world that folds in on itself.** Interconnected zones, shortcuts that loop back to shrines and the hideout, vistas that preview what's ahead.

## The game in one paragraph
In the far past of a mythic spirit world, a young woman whose family was killed by soul-devouring monsters steals the first soul sword from the guardian order. Sealed inside is a world-ending cat spirit that will devour her soul if she dies. Hunted by the guardians and the monsters drawn to the sword, she fights the cat for control, tames it at the midpoint, and learns its leaking power has been summoning the monsters. The cat sacrifices itself, splitting into ten shards that create the first ten captains.

## Not this game
- Not a stat-grind RPG or a loot game.
- Not a stealth-*focused* game: stealth is the guardian half, combat is the monster half.
- Not Bleach: no Bleach names, terms or character designs (see `NAMING.md`).
